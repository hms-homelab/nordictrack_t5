Treadmill Bridge

NordicTrack / iFit treadmill BLE to MQTT, with Home Assistant discovery. Flash an ESP32-S3 or ESP32-C3 straight from this page.

1. Flash

Plug the board in over USB, then press the button. The right image for your chip (S3 or C3) is picked automatically.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

If the board doesn't show up, hold its BOOT button while plugging it in.

2. Set it up

  1. After flashing, the board opens a WiFi network called Treadmill-Setup.
  2. Join it from your phone; the setup page opens by itself.
  3. Pick your WiFi, then enter your MQTT broker (host, port, user, password).
  4. It reboots, finds the treadmill over Bluetooth, and Home Assistant discovers an iFit Treadmill device.

To get back to setup later, hold BOOT while powering it on.

Source, enclosure and docs: github.com/hms-homelab/nordictrack_t5
